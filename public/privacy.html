Privacy in Honeyhollow

Honeyhollow is free. There are no ads, no purchases, no trackers, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. Nothing about you is sold or shared.

Playing as a guest

If you never create an account, everything stays in your own browser (its "local storage") and never leaves your device:

  • your city save and one backup copy of it,
  • your settings (sound, music, volume, motion, colours, sharp zoom, day and night, hints),
  • a random id for this browser, used only to recognise your own cloud saves if you later sign in.

To remove it: Settings → Start a new city, or clear this site's data in your browser.

If you create an account (optional)

An account lets you keep your city in the cloud and play with neighbors. We never ask for your email address or real name. The server keeps:

  • your username, the portrait you picked and your town's name,
  • your password and your recovery code, stored only as secure one-way hashes (nobody can read them back),
  • your friend code and the list of neighbors you added,
  • your city save, your level and experience (for the weekly leaderboard among your neighbors),
  • helps, gifts and job requests you send or receive (removed after 30 days),
  • the franchises you run in neighbors' towns or host in yours, while they are open,
  • when you last played (to sort neighbor lists).

Two small cookies make sign-in work: hh_session keeps you signed in (up to 30 days of inactivity), and hh_pre protects the sign-in forms against forged requests. Both are "HttpOnly" (pages and scripts can't read them) and are only sent to this game's own server.

To stop abuse, the server counts requests for a short time. It never stores your IP address: it keeps a scrambled, one-way fingerprint of it in those counters, and they are deleted within two days.

Deleting your account

Settings → Account → Delete my account deletes your account, cloud save, neighbor links, helps, gifts and franchises from the server at once. The city saved in your browser stays, as a guest city.

Who runs this game

Honeyhollow is run by the owner of this website. If you have a question about your data, contact them using the contact details on this site.
